REQUIRED INTERVIEW MONITORING

Event-based integrity snapshots

During the assessment, a still image from your live webcam (when available) and shared-screen stream will be captured when you switch browser tabs, exit fullscreen, move focus away from the interview window, or a camera/screen stream stops. If no webcam is available, only actual shared-screen frames can be captured. Reviewers can view these images. They are retained for up to {{evidenceRetentionDays}} days, then deleted. The assessment requests fullscreen, but browsers cannot prevent tab or app switching.

Acknowledgement required

Camera, mic and screen access require browser permission. Some system policies may block screen capture. The native integrity agent is not implemented.

Interview Rules

Work independently. Do not use ChatGPT, other copilots or external help. Keep the required capture streams enabled and stay in fullscreen during the assessment.

Monitoring Scope

No continuous video or audio is recorded. During the assessment, still images from your live webcam (when available) and shared-screen stream will be captured when you switch browser tabs, exit fullscreen, move focus away from the interview window, or a camera/screen stream stops. Fullscreen exits are recorded, but browsers cannot lock tabs or other apps. If no webcam is available, only actual shared-screen frames can be captured. Reviewers can view the images; they are retained for up to {{evidenceRetentionDays}} days and then deleted. These technical events are not proof of misconduct.
